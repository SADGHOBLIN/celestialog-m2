About

celestialog: a moonlit space for daily reflection.

Celestialog helps you slow down, organise your thoughts, and return to yourself through writing.

The unique Tarot Advisor system is a small council of guides imbued with personality, inspired by tarot archetypes.

  1. Track the phases of the moon
  2. Choose your advisor and consult your council
  3. Reflect in your journal

Personas

The Alchemist Card

A calm, playful guide who helps you turn uncertainty into something usable. Their presence makes journaling feel less like pressure, and more like possibility.

  • Style: mystical and whimsical
  • Gives: prompts, gentle reframes, and creative tools
  • When to use: when you feel stuck, stale, or unsure where to start

The Watcher Card

A quiet witness who helps you see the moment clearly, and notices blind spots without forcing comfort or closure.

  • Style: quiet, deliberate, and unsentimental
  • Gives: observations and questions that reveal what is being avoided
  • When to use: when you need help noticing patterns, or an accurate reflection of truth

The Logician Card

A direct, disciplined thinker who helps you organise your mind. They break problems into parts, and guide you towards clear, structured decisions.

  • Style: structured and pragmatic
  • Gives: step-by-step thinking, and clear options
  • When to use: when you need to plan, make a decision, or untangle a problem
